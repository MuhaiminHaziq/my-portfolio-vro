Attack & Defence

22 August 2026

We kicked off the first day with an engaging introduction to Attack & Defense CTF—a category I was completely new to, from the core mechanics and offensive/defensive strategies down to how the scoring engine calculates points.

To set the stage, Raihan walked us through the overall concept. Attack & Defense generally operates under two modes: Player vs. Player (PvP) and Player vs. Environment/Game Server (PvE). In a PvP format, teams simultaneously attack opponent infrastructure to capture flags while aggressively defending and patching their own systems against rival exploits. A particularly critical scenario involves backdoor persistence: if Team A implants a backdoor inside Team B's system, they can maintain unauthorized access even after the primary vulnerability has been patched.

Beyond strategy, we gained hands-on experience identifying security flaws and applying immediate remediation. This covered practical technical workflows, including working with Docker commands, managing containers, and inspecting environment images to keep services secure and operational under live fire.

Cryptography

23 August 2026

The second day shifted our focus toward Cryptography, and to be honest, I wasn't expecting it to be so mathematically intense. At times, the session felt more like an advanced mathematics lecture than a CTF training.

While working through heavy theoretical proofs can feel dry, I gained a much deeper appreciation and conceptual understanding of modern ciphers. It was fascinating to see how the mathematics from my previous semesters connects directly to cryptography. Seeing abstract concepts like Linear Algebra matrix transformations and Number Theory's modular arithmetic in action made me appreciate how formal mathematical structures form the backbone of modern encryption and key exchange mechanisms.

On top of that, we explored various encryption algorithms, modular arithmetic problems, and foundational theorems. Breaking down these mathematical principles gave me the confidence to recognize patterns and identify which formulas or attacks apply when tackling future CTF crypto challenges.
