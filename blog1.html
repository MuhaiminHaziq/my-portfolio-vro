Universiti Malaya Cybersecurity Summit 2026

Had the incredible opportunity to attend the UM Cybersecurity Summit, where I immersed myself in hands-on technical workshops and a panel discussion featuring top cybersecurity leaders. Events like this are always a great way to bridge theoretical knowledge with real-world industry practices. Here is a breakdown of what I learned and experienced throughout the day:

🛠️ Workshop 1: The Cyber Edge — Defense Tactics, Incident Response, and Career Growth

Hosted by LGMS Berhad

This session dived deep into how real-world cyberattacks actually unfold. We covered critical security concepts like attacker dwell time and the initial access vectors attackers use to compromise enterprise systems.

  • Digital Forensics vs. Incident Response: Breaking down the subtle yet crucial differences in how teams analyze evidence versus containing live threats.
  • Career Growth & Interview Prep: Actionable tips on how aspiring professionals can stand out during technical interviews and build a compelling profile in the cybersecurity field.

🔍 Workshop 2: Connecting the Dots through Log Analysis

Hosted by eclogic

Logs are the ultimate paper trail in network defense. This workshop provided hands-on exposure to parsing and evaluating diverse log formats to trace malicious behavior.

  • Pattern Recognition: Identifying malicious behavior across raw log files and structured event formats.
  • Investigative Support: Understanding how structured log analysis directly supports incident investigations and threat hunting.

🎙️ Industry Panel Forum: AI, Strategy & The Future of Cyber in Malaysia

Featuring Mr. Lucas (LGMS Berhad) & Mr. JP (VSTECS KU Sdn Bhd)

The event concluded with an insightful panel discussion on the evolving state of cybersecurity in Malaysia and expectations for future security analysts.

  • AI in Security: While AI tools accelerate threat detection and automated responses, human intuition, context, and decision-making remain completely irreplaceable.
  • Employer Expectations: Practical advice on the core skill sets, adaptability, and mindset that top cybersecurity companies look for in candidates today.
